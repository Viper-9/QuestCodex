# Quest guide
{: .no_toc }

Hit the **Guide** button on a quest to see what to bring before a raid. The same button sits in an expanded quest
in the Progress pages.

1. TOC
{:toc}

---

## What it shows

- The map the quest takes place on
- Who counts for kill and hit objectives: any PMC, Scavs, or specific bosses and guards
- Allowed weapons, calibers and mods
- Required or banned gear
- Items to hand over or plant, with found-in-raid requirements
- Rules like finishing in a single raid or using specific exits

Mod items are included too.

[![Items to plant](../assets/images/wiki-guide-plant.png)](../assets/images/wiki-guide-plant.png)

Long lists collapse to the first few entries. Click **+N more** to see them all.

[![Allowed weapons](../assets/images/wiki-guide-weapons.png)](../assets/images/wiki-guide-weapons.png)

## Kill targets

Kill and hit objectives list the enemies that count under **Kill targets** or **Hit targets**. "Any Scav-side AI"
includes bosses, guards, Raiders and Rogues, the same way the game counts them.

[![Kill targets for The Punisher - Part 6](../assets/images/wiki-guide-enemies.png)](../assets/images/wiki-guide-enemies.png)

## Moving and resizing

- Drag the header to move the popup.
- Drag any edge to resize it.
- Your layout is remembered. Double-click the header to reset it.
